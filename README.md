# Tuesday 24 November 2026 — The World in One Page

50 sourced stories, a UTC timeline and a date check — 24 November 2026.

One dense broadsheet page for Tuesday 24 November 2026: a navy masthead with the date and the region counts (Europe 15, Middle East 9, Asia 7, Africa 5, Americas 12, Oceania 1, Space 1), a UTC timeline with six slots from overnight to evening, four hero cards (Sanofi–Insmed, NATO and space, Gaza, Asia markets) with simple geometric illustrations, twelve smaller cards for stories 5–16 with thumbnails, a numbered world map with pins 1–50 and a seven-region legend, one-line briefs for stories 17–50 in two columns, and a footer DATE CHECK listing the stories that broke before Tuesday and were left out on purpose.

## How the stories were chosen

All stories were checked against live reporting dated Tuesday 24 November 2026. Claims from a single unconfirmed source are tagged [CLAIM]. Stories that broke earlier — Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO, the COP31 collapse, the CrowdStrike–Zscaler deal, and the others listed in the footer — are not presented as Tuesday news; they are named in the DATE CHECK instead.

## How to run

`python3 main.py` — pure Python stdlib, NO Pillow, no network at runtime, writes exactly one file `/output/entry.png` (PNG 1024x1024, under 1 MiB), cleans only the contents of `/output`, 2 runs byte-identical.
